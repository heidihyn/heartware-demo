How it ends

On the night, everyone is put into one of 5 small teams. A team is just a group to hunt with, not a side: each one mixes good guys, bad guys and lawyers. Each team's captain submits one suspect on this page. Whoever's named most is fired. Fire a Hacker and the company wins.
